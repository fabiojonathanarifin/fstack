Before doing anything else, restate in your own words:

1. The goal: what the user wants to be true when this is done.
2. The problem behind it: why they want it, inferred from the product and the conversation, not only the literal ask.
3. The smallest change that would meet it.
4. Anything you are unsure about or assuming.

Keep it to a few short lines. Then stop and wait for the user to confirm or correct you. Do not start the work in the same reply.
